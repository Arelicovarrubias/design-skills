# Stakeholder Updates

Use this when a designer needs to explain process, progress, decisions, evidence, risk, or requests to PMs, engineers, leadership, clients, or cross-functional partners.

## Update Principles

- Lead with the decision or action needed.
- Separate progress from evidence and opinion.
- Name risks without making the update feel blocked unless it is blocked.
- Keep asks concrete: decision, feedback, access, approval, time, or tradeoff.
- Match detail to audience.

## Tone Guidance

- Use calm, plain language.
- Avoid process jargon unless the audience uses it.
- Use evidence strength words: directional, strong signal, mixed signal, unresolved, decided.
- Do not overstate research confidence from small samples.
