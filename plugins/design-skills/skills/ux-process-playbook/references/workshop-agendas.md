# Workshop Agendas

Use this when a designer needs alignment, prioritization, assumption mapping, journey mapping, or collaborative concept generation.

## Workshop Decision Rules

- Use a workshop only when shared understanding or group decision-making is the blocker.
- Keep the invitation list to people who bring evidence, constraints, or decision authority.
- Define the decision before defining activities.
- End with owners, decisions, unresolved questions, and next steps.

## Alignment Workshop

Best for unclear goals, conflicting expectations, or drifting scope.

Agenda:

1. Goal and decision needed.
2. Current understanding: users, business goal, constraints.
3. Known evidence and missing evidence.
4. Assumptions by category: user, business, technical, operational.
5. Risks and confidence.
6. Decisions, owners, and next steps.

Output:

- Shared problem statement.
- Assumption map.
- Decision log.
- Next-step plan.

## Assumption Mapping

Best for deciding what to validate first.

Agenda:

1. List assumptions silently.
2. Group by user, value, feasibility, viability, and usability.
3. Plot by risk and evidence strength.
4. Select the top assumptions to test.
5. Choose validation methods and owners.

Output:

- Prioritized assumptions.
- Validation plan.
- Decision checkpoint.

## Journey Mapping

Best for complex services, cross-channel flows, or unclear current-state pain.

Agenda:

1. Define persona or role and scenario.
2. Map stages and user actions.
3. Add goals, questions, emotions, and pain points.
4. Add backstage systems, constraints, or ownership.
5. Identify moments that matter and opportunities.
6. Prioritize opportunities.

Output:

- Current or future journey.
- Pain points.
- Opportunity areas.
- Priority recommendations.

## Design Studio

Best for exploring multiple solution directions after the problem is clear.

Agenda:

1. Recap problem, users, constraints, and success criteria.
2. Individual sketching.
3. Share concepts.
4. Critique against criteria.
5. Combine strongest ideas.
6. Decide next prototype direction.

Output:

- Concept directions.
- Selection rationale.
- Prototype plan.
