# Discovery Plan Template

Use this when a designer needs to turn a vague product ask, stakeholder request, or feature idea into a practical discovery plan.

## Inputs To Gather

- Original ask or feature idea.
- Business goal or decision deadline.
- Target users or suspected audiences.
- Known data, research, support tickets, sales input, analytics, or prior decisions.
- Constraints: technical, legal, operational, content, design system, timeline.
- Stakeholders and decision-maker.

## Plan Structure

**Discovery goal**
State what the team needs to understand before committing to a direction.

**Key questions**
List 3-6 questions. Prefer questions that change decisions.

**Assumptions**
Separate user, business, technical, and delivery assumptions.

**Evidence plan**
Name the lightest activities that can answer the questions:

- Stakeholder interview.
- Existing research or analytics review.
- Customer/support signal review.
- User interview.
- Journey or task mapping.
- Competitive or pattern scan.
- Assumption mapping.

**Participants**
Name roles, not only people: PM, engineering lead, designer, researcher, support, sales, compliance, customer, end user.

**Timeline**
Break into short phases: gather, align, synthesize, decide.

**Output**
Choose one or two: problem statement, opportunity brief, assumptions map, research questions, concept directions, decision log.

## Quality Bar

- The plan should reveal what would change the team's decision.
- The plan should avoid asking users what stakeholders can answer.
- The plan should avoid treating stakeholder opinions as user evidence.
- The plan should end with a decision checkpoint, not only a readout.
