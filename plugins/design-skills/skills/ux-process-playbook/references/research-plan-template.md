# Research Plan Template

Use this for usability tests, concept tests, interviews, diary studies, survey planning, or lightweight validation.

## Research Plan Shape

**Objective**
State the decision the research should inform.

**Research questions**
Use 3-5 questions that map to design, product, or launch decisions.

**Hypotheses or assumptions**
List what the team currently believes and what would change the direction.

**Method**
Choose the lightest method that can answer the questions:

- Usability test: when task completion, comprehension, or flow quality is uncertain.
- Concept test: when value proposition, mental model, or direction is uncertain.
- Interview: when needs, context, motivation, or workflow are unclear.
- Survey: when sizing known patterns, not discovering unknown ones.
- Heuristic review: when user access is unavailable and the artifact needs fast expert feedback.

**Participants**
Define participant criteria, exclusions, sample size, and recruitment source.

**Stimulus**
Name what participants will see: mockup, prototype, concept card, existing flow, script, or task.

**Session outline**
Include warmup, tasks or prompts, follow-up probes, and closing questions.

**Analysis plan**
Name how findings will become decisions: severity ratings, theme clustering, opportunity mapping, recommendation matrix, or decision log.

**Timeline and owners**
Include preparation, sessions, synthesis, readout, and design revision.

## Quality Bar

- Ask about behavior, interpretation, and tradeoffs. Avoid asking participants to design the solution.
- Keep tasks realistic and outcome-based.
- Separate observed issues from participant preferences.
- Tie every research question to a decision.
