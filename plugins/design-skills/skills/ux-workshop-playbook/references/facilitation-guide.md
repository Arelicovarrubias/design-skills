# Facilitation Guide

Use this for workshop agendas, facilitation scripts, prompts, timeboxes, participant prep, and live capture plans.

## Agenda Structure

1. Frame the purpose and decision.
2. Share context and constraints.
3. Align on criteria or questions.
4. Run the core activity.
5. Cluster, compare, or vote.
6. Decide or identify unresolved questions.
7. Assign owners and next steps.

## Facilitation Roles

- Facilitator: guides flow, timeboxes, and participation.
- Decision owner: confirms decisions and tradeoffs.
- Scribe: captures decisions, open questions, and action items.
- Evidence holder: brings research, data, customer signal, or operational context.
- Technical or operational partner: names constraints and dependencies.
- Observer: watches patterns without steering the group.

## Prompt Patterns

Clarify:

- "What must be true for this direction to work?"
- "Where are we making assumptions?"
- "What evidence do we have, and what is still opinion?"

Diverge:

- "What are all the possible ways this could work?"
- "What would we try if the constraint disappeared?"
- "What would a simpler version look like?"

Converge:

- "Which option best meets the criteria?"
- "What is the riskiest unresolved assumption?"
- "What can we decide today?"

Close:

- "What did we decide?"
- "What remains open?"
- "Who owns the next action?"
- "What should be shared with stakeholders?"

## Capture Plan

Capture:

- Decisions.
- Rationale.
- Dissent or tension.
- Assumptions.
- Evidence references.
- Open questions.
- Action items.
- Parking lot items.
- Notable quotes when available.

Create a dedicated board area for:

- Raw input.
- Clusters.
- Decisions.
- Risks.
- Actions.
- Questions.

## Facilitation Standards

- State whether the workshop is for alignment, generation, evaluation, or decision-making.
- Do not let voting replace decision ownership.
- Keep silent writing before group discussion when power dynamics matter.
- Timebox debate and capture unresolved tensions visibly.
- End with decisions and owners, even if the decision is to gather more evidence.
